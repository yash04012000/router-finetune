Step 3.2 · Tokenizer: what the model receives

    
The model cannot read words. The tokenizer cuts the message into pieces from a 30,522-piece vocabulary and gives each an id.
      [CLS] and [SEP] are added automatically; a dotted edge marks a piece that continues the previous one (##).

    


    
Step 3.3 · The untrained model: from tokens to 16 scores

    
The pretrained body turns every token into 768 numbers. The new, random head reads the [CLS] vector and gives one score per intent.
